## Accessibility

Pa11y CI checked seven pages at a 390 × 844 mobile viewport against WCAG 2 AA. All seven pages passed with zero reported issues.

## Links

The calibrated link scan checked 508 references and returned zero broken-link errors. Login-only or bot-blocked LinkedIn, Udemy and Instagram URLs are excluded from automated status checks.

One actionable redirect remains:

- `https://github.com/UAJOP/Wheather-App` → `https://github.com/UAJOP/Weather-App`

## HTML priorities

The validator separates structural errors from non-blocking semantic warnings. Highest-priority findings are:

1. Close the unmatched wrapper `<section>` in `works.html`.
2. Remove empty image `src` values used by modal templates, or create those images dynamically.
3. Prevent focusable controls from remaining inside containers marked `aria-hidden="true"`.
4. Give repeated landmark regions unique accessible names.
5. Encode the raw ampersand reported in `ai-flow-puzzle.html`.

Warnings about `aria-label` usage and clickable `article` elements should be addressed gradually as component-level refactors.

## Rollout

This baseline is report-first and does not block deployment. After the structural findings are fixed, selected checks can be promoted to required pull-request gates.
