Business Context & Challenge

Turning a creative studio into a clear digital customer journey.

Atölye Joyday needed a digital presence that could explain several different creative experiences without making the customer journey feel fragmented. The website was designed not only as a presentation layer, but also as the main entry point for reservation requests, workshop applications and direct communication.

Customers needed to understand experiences, package options and participation formats, while the business needed more structured request collection and notification support.

My Role

Co-Founder & Digital Product Developer

Web Development, Reservation Flow Design & Digital Operations

As Co-Founder and Digital Product Developer, I designed and developed the website structure, responsive interface, service and package presentation, reservation entry points and operational form workflows. I also built Google Forms, Sheets and Apps Script based automations for structured submissions and email notifications, while supporting the business’s digital visibility and day-to-day operational needs.

Reservation Journey

From service discovery to a structured reservation request.

The journey supports three public entry channels while keeping availability, prepayment and final confirmation in direct business communication.

  1. 1Discover an experience
  2. 2Review details and packages
  3. 3Choose a reservation channel
  4. 4Share reservation information
  5. 5Receive operational follow-up
  6. 6Complete confirmation and prepayment communication

Structured request information

Name, phone, email, experience or package, participant count, date, time range, photo/video consent, privacy acceptance and notes are collected with field validation where relevant.

Operational Automation

Connecting customer requests with lightweight operational automation.

The operational workflow uses Google Forms to collect structured requests, Google Sheets to record submissions and Google Apps Script to trigger email notifications. Separate reservation and workshop application paths help the business receive the right information before continuing the conversation manually.

Testing, Operations & Limitations

Built for real business use, with clear operational boundaries.

Verified testing areas

  • Desktop, tablet and mobile responsive checks
  • Navigation, public CTA and external-link tests
  • Form validation and reservation-flow tests
  • Workshop application and Google Sheets recording tests
  • Date, time and closed-day rule tests
  • Business and customer notification email tests
  • Asset optimization checks

Operational limitations

  • This is not a real-time availability system.
  • Online payment processing is not included.
  • There are no customer accounts.
  • Final reservation confirmation continues through business communication.
  • The workflow depends on Google Forms, Sheets and Apps Script.
  • Business and customer records are excluded from this case study for privacy.
  • Live prices and service details may change over time.
