Client need

Make the work feel architectural, not templated.

The client needed a premium digital presence for prospective clients and collaborators. The interface had to protect the composition of project photography, support complete localized routes and guide visitors from discovery to a professional inquiry without turning the site into a generic corporate template.

Role & collaboration

Product decisions, implementation, QA and release coordination.

I translated client feedback into the information architecture, responsive interaction model and production implementation; coordinated content and delivery decisions; and owned QA and release approval. ChatGPT, Claude and Codex assisted parts of the engineering workflow. The interior projects, photography and founder imagery remain the work of Atelis and their respective rights holders.

UX & art direction

An editorial frame that stays out of the photography's way.

Warm neutrals, restrained typography, controlled whitespace and subtle motion keep attention on the interiors. Desktop compositions use generous image-led grids; mobile layouts reorganize the same content without stretching narrow captures into landscape frames.

Portfolio system

Four real projects, honest placeholders and reusable galleries.

L'Inconnue, Maison Elan, Walden and Casa Olivia are the published projects. The data-driven grid adds two clearly labelled Coming Soon cards in the All view, supports category filters and counts, and gives real projects localized detail pages with responsive galleries and a keyboard-operable lightbox.

Multilingual architecture

Seven complete Atelis locales with German as the default entry.

DE, EN, TR, FR, ES, IT and RU use explicit localized URLs. Route helpers keep equivalent paths aligned while canonical and hreflang metadata identify each real language page. This portfolio case study itself follows kaanbalci.com's separate five-language contract.

Inquiry workflow

A lightweight server-side path from form to business inbox.

The localized contact form posts to a Cloudflare Pages Function, which validates the request and uses Resend for business notifications. One controlled release test returned HTTP 200 and delivery to the business inbox and CC recipient was confirmed by the site owner; no form is submitted from this case study.

Engineering tradeoffs

Static-first delivery, typed content and an explicit CMS boundary.

Astro and typed content records keep public pages fast and predictable. Project grid rules are isolated behind 14 permanent algorithm tests. New projects currently require a rebuild and release: there is no live client-managed CMS, dashboard, quotation system or payment flow.

QA & outcome

Released with documented multi-locale and responsive evidence.

The delivered site builds 65 HTML pages, including 63 intended indexable localized URLs. Release records report 14 grid tests, 67 functional interaction checks, 16 contact-function mocks, and 588 plus 168 viewport checks. These are reported release checks, not independently re-run benchmarks, and no lead, conversion or ranking uplift is claimed.
