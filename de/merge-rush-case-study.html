Product problem

Merge-Entscheidungen sollen mehr bedeuten als das Zusammenführen zweier gleicher Teile.

Der frühe Prototyp bewies die Merge-Interaktion, doch das Board ließ sich mit zu wenig Platzverbrauch abschließen. Das senkte den Druck zum Platzmanagement und machte die Progression flach.

Der Neuentwurf verbindet Spielfeldplatz, zeitgebundene Aufträge und Fabrik-Restaurierung zu einer Produktschleife, statt die Punktzahl als einziges Ziel zu behandeln.

Design goal

Readable in seconds, deeper over the full run.

The player should immediately understand drop → merge → order, then discover a second layer of larger footprints, staged board unlocks, repair progression, combos and failure pressure.

Core loop

Short-term orders power long-term restoration.

  1. 01Drop parts from the next queue
  2. 02Merge matching tiers
  3. 03Fulfill the active timed order
  4. 04Earn score, combo and Repair Energy
  5. 05Restore the next factory stage
  6. 06Unlock more board space and higher pressure

Gameplay engineering

Der Platzdruck auf dem Board ist als echter Zustand und echte Platzierungslogik umgesetzt.

Multi-cell occupancy

Higher-tier pieces use 2×1, 1×2 and 2×2 footprints. Placement, movement, merge, delivery and deadlock checks are footprint-aware.

Preview-before-commit

Drag/drop validates the destination before committing board state, keeping visual feedback and logical placement aligned.

Deadlock awareness

The run checks whether legal placements or merges remain instead of treating a visually crowded board as automatically terminal.

Endless repair loop

Wrong-action damage and successful-delivery repair create a second failure/recovery loop after the main Factory Run.

Progression

Die Fabrik selbst wird zur sichtbaren Fortschrittsanzeige des Durchlaufs.

01 — Emergency Power

Compact starting area makes early placement matter.

02 — Assembly Line

More board capacity arrives as order pressure grows.

03 — Generator

Mid-run restoration becomes a visible pacing checkpoint.

04 — Robotics Bay

Larger items and board planning become more important.

05 — Factory Core

The final restoration stage anchors the high-tier end of the run.

Production architecture

Spielregeln werden von Plattformbelangen getrennt.

PlatformAdapter

Local and YouTube adapters isolate host-platform behavior from core gameplay.

Lifecycle & readiness

Platform lifecycle and readiness coordination avoid scattering host callbacks through scene code.

Save / audio / language

Save, audio and localization concerns use dedicated abstractions rather than being embedded in GameScene.

Pause-aware loading

Boot/loading architecture accounts for lifecycle interruptions and production platform behavior.
