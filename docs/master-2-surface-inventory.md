## Cross-surface findings

- Current public pages use many near-duplicate translucent surfaces, radii, shadows, and synthetic font weights.
- Hero headings are consistently larger than the content hierarchy needs, especially at 320–430px.
- Dark mode has stronger contrast and identity; light mode relies too heavily on pale gradients and translucent white.
- Primary and archive work are semantically separated but not always visually separated enough.
- Existing project and case-study media is sufficient; no generated or stock evidence is needed.
- No factual conflict was found between the visible page copy and canonical portfolio data during the inventory. Claims that lacked an established outcome were flagged for bounded wording rather than upgraded.
